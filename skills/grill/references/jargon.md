# Abbreviations and jargon

`aayu grill <type> --assess` lists every abbreviation, numeronym, and listed term in a request that is not on the
Plain list. Before you write anything, give each one its full meaning, ask the human to confirm it, and record it
in Provenance as `Term: "<abbreviation>" → <meaning>; "<the human's words>"`. Ask about an unknown term ("What do
you mean by RFQ here?"). Never guess one. A listed term with two or more meanings is the riskiest kind, so ask
about it first. This file is data: add a term when a request trips on one.

The Plain terms are never unpacked, because readers of a software repository already share them.

Plain: API, APIs, CLI, UI, UX, URL, URLs,
JSON, CSV, PDF, HTML, CSS, HTTP, HTTPS, SQL, ID, IDs, OK, PR, PRs, CI, README, TOML, YAML, XML, iOS, AI, US, UK, EU,
AM, PM, TODO, MUST, NOT, NEVER, ALWAYS, ALL, NO, NOW, NEW, FREE, ONLY, DO, IS, IT, OR, AND

## Terms

- 2FA: two-factor authentication
- A11Y: accessibility
- ASAP: as soon as possible (a date the human has not named)
- B2B: business to business (orders from companies) | a business-customer portal
- B2C: business to consumer
- CAC: customer acquisition cost
- CLS: Cumulative Layout Shift
- CMS: content management system
- CRM: customer relationship management
- CRUD: create, read, update, and delete screens or endpoints
- CTA: call to action (the main button or link)
- CWV: Core Web Vitals (LCP, INP, CLS)
- DAU: daily active users
- DX: developer experience
- E2E: end-to-end (a test that drives the whole product)
- EOD: end of day
- EOW: end of week
- ETA: estimated time of arrival | estimated completion date
- FAQ: frequently asked questions
- GDPR: the EU General Data Protection Regulation
- I18N: internationalisation (more than one language or locale)
- INP: Interaction to Next Paint
- KPI: key performance indicator
- L10N: localisation (translated text and local formats)
- LCP: Largest Contentful Paint
- MAU: monthly active users
- MFA: multi-factor authentication
- MVP: minimum viable product (the smallest version worth releasing)
- NPS: Net Promoter Score
- OKR: objectives and key results
- OOTB: out of the box (works without configuration)
- P&L: profit and loss statement
- PDP: product detail page | personal development plan
- PII: personally identifiable information
- PLP: product listing page
- POC: proof of concept
- POS: point of sale (the till)
- PWA: progressive web app (installable, works offline)
- RBAC: role-based access control
- RFQ: request for quote
- ROI: return on investment
- SaaS: software as a service
- SEO: search engine optimisation
- SKU: stock keeping unit (one sellable product variant)
- SLA: service level agreement
- SLO: service level objective
- SMB: small and medium-sized businesses
- SSO: single sign-on
- TTFB: time to first byte
- TTI: time to interactive
- UAT: user acceptance testing
- WCAG: Web Content Accessibility Guidelines
- WIP: work in progress
