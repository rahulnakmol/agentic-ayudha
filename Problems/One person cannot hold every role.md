# One person cannot hold every role

## The problem

One or two people cannot be the strategist, the architect, the engineer, and the reviewer at once.
They also cannot be the release engineer and the person on call.
Most products outgrow their process before they outgrow their code.

## Who has it

Founders and small teams who build with agents.
See [[The solo founder]].

## Evidence

The team concepts page and the growth guide state it.
The usual fixes are more meetings, more handovers, and more people in the loop.
See [[Seats and personas]].
