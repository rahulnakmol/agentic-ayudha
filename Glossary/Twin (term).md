# Twin

## Meaning

A twin is the agent half of a seat.
A seat is solo when only its twin works in it.
A seat is paired when a person works in it too.
Each twin has a persona with a name, a voice, and a practice playbook.

## Not to be confused with

A twin is not a person, and it never approves.
A twin never fakes or implies an approval that did not happen.
See [[Seat (term)]].
