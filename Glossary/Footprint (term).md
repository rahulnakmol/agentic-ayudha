# Footprint

## Meaning

A footprint is the set of layers that a Story or a pull request touches.
aayu computes it from the changed paths.

## Not to be confused with

A footprint is not a risk tier.
The risk tier uses the footprint as one of its inputs.
verify uses it to choose floors, and slice records it for each Story.
See [[Layer (term)]].
