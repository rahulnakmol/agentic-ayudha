# Ready

## Meaning

Ready is the stage at which a work item passes validation.
aayu ready posts a Ready snapshot with a hash of the body and of the Completion Goal.
Later checks compare against that snapshot.

## Not to be confused with

Ready is not done.
Ready freezes the promise, and the proof pack later shows it was kept.
See [[Completion Goal (term)]].
