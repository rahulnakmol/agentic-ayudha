# Seat

## Meaning

A seat is one of fourteen roles in the pod, such as Product Strategist or Code Architect.
Every verb has one Accountable seat.
Every layer has one owner seat.
A seat outlives the person who fills it.

## Not to be confused with

A seat is not a person.
A person pairs with a seat, and an agent twin always works in it.
See [[Twin (term)]].
