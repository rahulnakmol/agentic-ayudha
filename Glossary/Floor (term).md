# Floor

## Meaning

A floor is a deterministic check with a threshold.
Examples are coverage, SAST, secrets, dependencies, accessibility, and performance.
A miss blocks the merge.
The defaults are in CONSTRAINTS.md.

## Not to be confused with

A floor is not a model's opinion.
No disposition can override a floor.
Only a waiver lets one miss through.
See [[Waiver (term)]].
