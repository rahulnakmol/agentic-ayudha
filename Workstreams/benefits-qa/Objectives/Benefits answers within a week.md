# Benefits answers within a week

## The objective

Employees of the fictional employer Example Co get benefits answers within a week of first asking.
Each answer quotes the plan documents, instead of waiting on an HR ticket.
Goal #125 states the outcome.
Initiative #126 is the self-serve way to reach it.
All data is synthetic, and no real person is measured.

## How we measure it

The metric is activation, as aayu measure computes it.
Activation is the share of synthetic employees with a grounded answer within 7 days.
The baseline was 0.258.
The target of Initiative #126 is at least 0.60.

## Why it matters

benefits-qa is the nine-layer example that the pod built from grill goal to release.
It shows the whole loop on one product.
See [[Grounded benefits answers]].
