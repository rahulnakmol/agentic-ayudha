# The solo founder

## Who they are

One person with a product to build.
An agent twin fills each of the fourteen seats around them.
They keep the steps that only a person may take.

## What they try to do

They want the interviewing, planning, building, checking, and reporting done without them.
They answer the interview, accept Decisions, ask for stable releases, and publish.
aayu inbox gathers most of their waiting steps into one list.

## What gets in the way

GitHub does not let them approve their own pull request.
So a protected-path change needs a recorded solo-mode bypass.
Every human-only step costs their attention.
See [[Review attention does not scale per pull request]].
