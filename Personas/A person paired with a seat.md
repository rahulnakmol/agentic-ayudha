# A person paired with a seat

## Who they are

A person who joins a pod and pairs with one or more seats.
One command pairs them: aayu seat add with their GitHub login.
The twin keeps doing the seat's work.

## What they try to do

They review changes in the layers that their seat owns.
Risky layers are where a person is needed most.
As the team grows, people go where a person is needed.

## What gets in the way

With two people in the repository, protected paths need a CODEOWNER's approval.
The solo-mode bypass is then refused.
See [[Seats and personas]].
