# Rubric: grill goal, first claim within a week

Score the Goal from 1 to 5. The request states: the outcome (more claimants finish their first claim within a week
of signing up), the beneficiary group (claimants), the metric (activation: first claim submitted within 7 days), the
baseline (0.31 over the weekly cohorts of 2026-08-31 to 2026-09-28, from an `aayu measure` snapshot), and the target
(0.40 or more by 2027-03-31). A Goal has no parent.

A baseline is reproducible when it keeps its value, its window or cohorts, and its source (the snapshot). A target is
usable when it keeps its value, its direction, and its date.

Questions the skill lists under "open questions" are not requirements: they never lower the score. Invented content
is anything the request does not state: another metric, a different baseline or target number, another date, a
beneficiary group of the skill's own, or initiatives and features the request does not ask for.

- 5: Summary states the outcome and who benefits; beneficiaries is claimants; the metric is activation as defined;
  the baseline keeps 0.31 with its cohorts and snapshot; the target keeps >= 0.40 by 2027-03-31; nothing invented.
- 4: As 5, with one minor gap, such as a summary that could say more plainly who benefits.
- 3: Usable, but the baseline lacks its window or source, or the target lacks its date or direction.
- 2: A changed or invented number, metric, or date, or the baseline and target are not comparable.
- 1: Not a usable Goal.
