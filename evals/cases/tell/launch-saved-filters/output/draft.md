# Your morning filters, saved and shared

For claims reviewers who rebuild the same filters by hand every morning and describe them to each other in chat,
Benefits QA now keeps those filters for you. Save a filter once, reopen it on any day, and share it with a link
([Saved filters, #15](https://github.com/acme/benefits-qa/issues/15)).

## Save it once

Set up the claims list the way you review it, by status and date range, and choose Save filter. Give it a name, and it
appears under Saved filters in the sidebar. Reopen it in a new session, and the list shows the claims that match its
conditions at that moment.

## Share it with a link

Copy link gives you a link that opens the claims list with the same filter for any signed-in reviewer. They can apply
your filter, but they cannot rename or delete it.

## Take the list with you

Need to audit a filtered list offline? Export CSV in the list toolbar downloads every claim your filters match
([#7](https://github.com/acme/benefits-qa/issues/7)). Since it shipped, reviewers have made 4.2 CSV exports per
reviewer per week, against a target of 3.

Open your claims list, apply the filter you rebuild every morning, and save it.
