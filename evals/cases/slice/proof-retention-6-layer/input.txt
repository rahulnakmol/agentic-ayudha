Slice Feature #40 (Proof retention per artifact kind).

Summary: Teams keep screenshots and recordings for a shorter time than reports, by setting a retention per artifact kind; purge honours it on a schedule.

Problem: Recordings fill the private media bucket: one retention for every kind keeps 90 days of video that nobody opens after a release, while auditors need reports for a year.

Scope: In scope: per-kind retention in config (migrating the single retention_days), purge by kind, a `proof purge --kind` flag, listing ledger packs through the API, and a scheduled purge workflow. Out of scope: changing the pack format.
