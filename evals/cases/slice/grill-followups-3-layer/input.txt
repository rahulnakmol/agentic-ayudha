Slice Feature #40 (Grill asks a follow-up when an answer is vague).

Summary: When a human gives a vague answer during `aayu grill`, the interview asks one follow-up question before moving on.

Scope: In scope: follow-up questions in the grill skill and the CLI interview, and eval cases for them. Out of scope: other verbs' interviews.
