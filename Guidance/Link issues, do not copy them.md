# Link issues, do not copy them

## The guidance

GitHub issues are the record of the work.
A note links an issue by its number, such as #593.
A note never copies an issue's body, criteria, options, discussion, status, or labels.
Write the durable reason in your own short words, and link the rest.

## When it applies

It applies to every note that cites a Goal, Initiative, Feature, Decision, or Release issue.
It matters most for Stories and Bugs, because Ready freezes their Completion Goal.
A copy in a note would drift from the snapshot.

## Examples

- An Objective note names Goal #29 under issues and says what the Goal means.
- A Decision note gives the context and the choice, and links Decision #593 for the options.
- A Capability note lists its Feature issues by number.
- See [[Completion Goal (term)]] and [[Ready (term)]].
