# Ship aayudha 0.1

## The objective

Founders and their paired people run an agent delivery pod from one static binary.
Seats, layers, and verbs take work from a grilled idea to a measured release.
Proof packs and human gates guard each step.
Goal #29 is the record of this work.

## How we measure it

We count the build plan's work packages merged to dev with their acceptance met.
The plan has 61 work packages, plus the M4.5 thin-slice run.
Goal #29 holds the baseline and the target.

## Why it matters

The 0.1 release is the first complete pod that a person can install.
Every later capability builds on it.
See [[Release and promotion]] and [[v0.1.0]].
