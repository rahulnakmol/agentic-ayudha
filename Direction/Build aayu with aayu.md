# Build aayu with aayu

## Where we are going

aayu builds the rest of aayu with its own process.
Each remaining work package of the build plan is a Story with a Completion Goal.
Each Story is made Ready, built, proven, and shipped through the same gates aayu gives its users.
The benefits-qa example in this repository shows the same loop on a nine-layer product.

## Why now

A tool that says "prove it" must prove itself.
Ground rule 1 of the build plan says the plan runs on its own process.
Goal #29 and Initiative #30 track this work.
See [[Ship aayudha 0.1]] and [[Prove every Story of aayu itself]].

## What we will not do

We will not count a Story as covered without an authoritative pack.
We will not build ahead of the plan.
A later feature stays "n/a until" its work package.
See [[Say what does not exist yet]].
