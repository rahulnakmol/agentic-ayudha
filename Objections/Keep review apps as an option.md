# Keep review apps as an option

## The concern

Decision #654 moved review into the delivery loop and dropped the review apps.
rahulnakmol objected to dropping them.
The apps can still do pull request reviews for users who want them.
They need not be the default, but their support should stay.

## Evidence

Decision #656 quotes this concern, dated 2026-10-18.
Its recommendation keeps the apps as an optional mode.
A comment on Decision #654 records that #656 superseded it.

## Resolution

The concern was accepted.
Decision #656 was accepted on 2026-10-18 with the apps as an optional mode.
The direction now keeps them.
See [[Review inside the delivery loop]].
