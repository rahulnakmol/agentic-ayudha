# D-5.6c

## Plan assumption

S-5.6c: "Decisions awaiting acceptance, including eval-bar waivers"; "Stories replaced by another Story ('Replaces #N') that are still open"; "Stories whose PR merged without a ship record"; "PRs waiting for a human merge because inspect hit its cycle cap"; "Bugs whose fix merged"; "with the twin's recommendation"; "Every write goes through aayu with the person's identity".

## Finding

No record says a Story was shipped other than its status (`ship` moves it to `status:merged`), no record links a Bug to a fix except the PR's link to it, and inspect's cap lives only in its replace-in-place comment's state. A Decision's recommendation is its own Recommendation field, not a twin's. Options are written either as `name: …` (the waiver form) or as numbered bold items with "(recommended)". Bug 417's fix landed in pull request 418, which links only Story 399, so no record says 417 is fixed.

## Implemented instead

`gatherInbox` (`internal/cli/inbox.go`) reads the repository's issues once (state all), its closed and open PRs, and the comments of the open PRs, and lists, in this order: open Decisions (provisional ones to ratify or revert, floor waivers named as such; one that is not Decision-ready has only open and skip); open non-draft PRs whose inspect state has `cycles >= inspect.MaxCycles` (recommendation: open it; another: `aayu ship`, with every gate of ship); open Stories before `status:merged` that a PR merged into dev links (a `story/<n>-` branch, or Refs, Closes, Fixes, or Resolves #n, `audit.PR.LinkStories`), recommended to be marked merged without proof as `audit --mark-merged` does, with the post-gate comment, under the person's identity, removing the build lease; open Bugs that such a merged PR links, recommended to be closed as completed with a "Fixed in #PR" comment and `status:done`; and open Stories or Bugs that another issue's body says it replaces ("Replaces #N"), recommended to be closed as not planned with a "Replaced by #M" comment and `status:cancelled`. A Decision's recommendation is its Recommendation field ("the Decision recommends"); the other kinds name their Accountable seat's twin (Metis for a replaced item, Argus for a merged Story, Nestor for a capped PR or a fixed Bug). Accept on a Decision runs `decideAccept`, so its authority, its options, and the provisional label's clearing are `decide`'s. Every other write uses the client from `humanCaller` (refused for a bot login) and is logged with identity `human`. `--dry-run` plans every write. A Bug whose fix PR does not link it is not listed (Bug 417): link it, or close it from GitHub with the evidence. Tests: `TestInbox` (refusals with no write; the six steps in order with their recommendations and two non-steps left out; accept on each with the resulting state, labels, comments, and identity of every logged write; skip writes nothing; choose another reverts a provisional Decision; the pre-tool hook blocks `aayu inbox`) and `TestInboxOnHome` (home and `next` say nothing of an empty inbox and point to it when it has a step)
