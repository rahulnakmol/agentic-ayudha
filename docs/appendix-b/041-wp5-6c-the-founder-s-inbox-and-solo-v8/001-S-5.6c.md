# S-5.6c

## Plan assumption

Every human-only step has its own verb and its own place: `aayu decide <n> --accept` for a Decision, `aayu ship` for a PR, `aayu audit --mark-merged` for a story merged without proof. Closing a replaced Story or a fixed Bug has no verb, so a person runs `gh issue close` (Appendix B D-3.2c.h, Story 399's hand-off).

## Finding

Founder request (2026-10-17, relayed by the orchestrating thread, "i totally support it and we should build it"): a "YOLO mode" so that the solo founder is not the bottleneck on human-only steps. A switch that turns human-only gates off was rejected, because in solo mode an agent could flip it through the admin bypass. The backlog that day: waiver Decision 413 and Decisions 283, 287, and 324; Stories 331 to 334, each replaced by another Story; Stories 397, 399, and 400, whose PRs merged without a ship record; PR 192 at inspect's cycle cap; and Bug 417.

## Implemented instead

Story 431: `aayu inbox`, a human-only plumbing command (Founder seat), refused for agents and without a terminal, and blocked by the host pre-tool hooks (`humanPlumbing`). It lists every human-only step with the twin's recommendation and acts on each with one keypress (accept, choose another, skip, open, quit). The home screen gains a "Waiting in your inbox" row, the status line an inbox count, and `aayu next` names `aayu inbox` before any other human step when it has steps. The inbox adds two human-only capabilities no verb had, closing a replaced Story and closing a fixed Bug, each under the person's own token and through the actions log; no gate is turned off.
