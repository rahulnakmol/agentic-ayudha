# D-5.9k

## Plan assumption

D-5.9i and the founder's follow-ups: the trap findings, the do-not-carry list, and the compatibility contract are scored by evals at 4.0 or more, like every survey case

## Finding

The survey cases had no runtime evidence: a host that ran `--probe` saw facts the eval host could not, and could not cite them

## Implemented instead

`[survey] probes` in a case lists `probes.json` files recorded by `aayu survey --golden … --run --probe` on the fixture's own legacy build; the host sees them as the CLI prints them, and `probe:` citations resolve (`internal/evals/survey.go`). Three cases score the new parts on two more stacks: `survey/minpack-traps` (fixed-form Fortran: the Release flags and an `-O1` sanitiser build change results, COMMON, work arrays, a contract for "the same results"), `survey/maruku-compat` (Ruby: a frozen-string-literal build fails all 20 block docs at four mutation sites, the Array patch, embedded Ruby, a contract with every level open), and `survey/maruku-backlog` (the backlog after a recorded interview: K1-K3 may differ, K4 dropped with a deprecation path, N1-N3 confirmed as Decisions constraining the Stories that port their code). Scope rules on a backlog skip a Decision's `options`, which name the alternatives it rejects (`TestSurveyFloors` holds a Story that carries a confirmed entry to the floor and the recorded Decisions as the control); and a question's `affects` may name suites, migrations, and shims. The fixtures are subsets with their licences (THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md), and the Maruku subset keeps `data/entities.xml`, which the renderer reads (`lib/maruku/output/entity_table.rb:14`) and whose absence failed the entities case
