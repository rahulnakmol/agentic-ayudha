# D-5.9e

## Plan assumption

Decision #287 and the founder's request: each new skill scores at least 4.0 of 5 in an advisory eval group (judge `gpt-6.1-sol`, k = 5, at least two realistic cases per skill, tier-1 floors for hard rules such as cited evidence and no invented requirements), without trimming output to pass

## Finding

A survey's input is a whole codebase, which no eval case can paste; a case built on a toy fixture would not be realistic; and no person is present in an eval to answer the interview

## Implemented instead

The skill body (`skills/survey/SKILL.md`, with `references/formats.md` and `references/modernisation.md`) gives the procedure: index, module packs, a draft at three levels with the basis rule, the interview in rounds of three to five questions with defaults, the backlog with review before filing, and golden capture with parity; it adds what the pilots taught (check stated claims against the code; probe an inferred claim when a probe is cheap). The `survey` case type (`internal/evals/survey.go`) runs on two real codebases kept as fixtures (`evals/cases/survey/repos`: tiny-js, C++, and Parsedown, PHP; THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md): each run indexes the fixture through `aayu survey` and every `--module`, as a host would, and gives the skill that text plus the files the case lists, numbered by line. Cases: `survey/tinyjs-rust` and `survey/parsedown-modern-php` (kind `draft`: no person in the session, so an answered question or a confirmed claim fails the floor) and `survey/tinyjs-backlog` (kind `backlog`: from a checked survey whose answered questions are a recorded interview). Floors before the judge: `survey.Check` or `survey.CheckBacklog` and the case's scope rules (a performance figure nothing measures; a fix for a quirk the person kept; limits for scripts the person called trusted), with one revision on their findings, as a host revises after the check. Group `survey` (30 USD, `gate = "advisory"`, dispatch only). Tests: `TestSurveyFloors` (each planted invention fails, the recorded replies pass), `TestTier2SurveyRevisesOnceThenJudges`, `TestGroups`. Tier-2 runs and their scores follow in D-5.9g
