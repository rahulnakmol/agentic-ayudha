# D-5.9c

## Plan assumption

Decision #287: the backlog never floods GitHub: drafts the person reviews before anything is filed, idempotent re-runs

## Finding

GitHub has no draft issue for a repository (Projects v2 draft items are views only, WP2.1), and a re-run must find what it filed before even when the local state is gone

## Implemented instead

`aayu survey --backlog backlog.json --draft survey.json` files nothing: it checks each item against the work-item schema at its stage (a Story or Bug at Ready with its Completion Goal and effort, a Feature at Ready with its problem and scope, a Goal or Initiative at Create, since their baselines come from `measure`, never from a guess), a legal tree (one Goal; a Story or Bug under a Feature), and its basis in the survey: an `unknown` claim or an open question never backs an item, a Goal needs a stated or confirmed product claim, a Story or Feature an engineering or capability claim, a Bug a known-issue marker or an observed or stated risk, and a modernisation Story names its parity case sets in its own Completion Goal. It writes `backlog.md`, the exact bodies in filing order, and a 16-hex review digest of every key, type, parent, link, title, label, and body. `--create --reviewed <digest>` files only that digest (exit 4 otherwise), at most `--limit` issues a run (default 25), through the actions log, as the person (a human run) or the bot (an agent lane, `identity:human-fallback` without one). Each issue carries the hidden line `<!-- aayudha:survey product=<slug> key=<key> -->` before its first section, where the parser does not read, and the label `survey:<slug>`; a run lists the label's issues and reads their markers, plus `filed.json` (written after every issue), so it skips what is filed, links new children under parents already filed, writes `depends-on #N` with the real numbers, and never edits an existing issue (`TestSurveyBacklogCreate`)
