# D-6.6a

## Plan assumption

WP6.6: lane A (Gemini 3.1 Pro / Fable 5.1 medium, `--deep` high) with Nielsen 10×0–4 and personas incl. screen-reader; lane B (impeccable `detect --json`, Apache-2.0, shelled out; axe); isolated; dispositions `recapture\|rebuild\|fix\|ship`; `polish` consumes the critique; `baseline` per SHA; lane A k = 3, a finding counts when ≥ 2 of 3 runs report it at severity ≥ 2; accept: three planted defects (missing focus ring, 3.8:1 body text, 32 px tap target) each caught by lane A in ≥ 2 of 3 runs and by lane B in 1 of 1, a clean fixture ships from both lanes, `polish` removes all three on re-run

## Finding

Built ahead of M4.5 and M6 (founder scheduling, 2026-10-12). The gateway serves only `gemini-3.1-pro-low` (D-1.6a), and Fable 5.1 is anthropic, so it is valid only when the builder is not. impeccable 4.5.2 on the planted page reports only the contrast defect: it has no rule for a missing focus indicator, and its touch-target rule did not fire on a 32 px button at 1280×800 or 390×844. axe's `target-size` is WCAG 2.5.8 (24 px), which 32 px passes, and axe has no focus-visible rule. The plan defines no matching of findings across runs, no disposition rules, and no source of the states. The model gateway sent text only. `polish` runs in the host (mode `host`), so its acceptance needs a host model

## Implemented instead

`internal/uireview` plus `review`, `polish`, and `baseline` handlers (`internal/cli/review.go`). States are the screenshot and recording criteria of the story's Ready snapshot on the `[verify.environments]` target (never production), and the checkout must be the PR head. The embedded Playwright script captures each state once at 1280×800 (a screenshot at rest and one after a Tab press, the markup without comments, and the accessibility tree), and both lanes judge that capture without seeing each other. Lane A is the ui-review lane resolved on the direct host (`gemini-3.1-pro-low` through the gateway), checked by `models.CheckFamilies` against the builder's family (the local pack's model, else the build lane on the host). It runs 3 times per state at effort `medium` (`high` with `--deep`), with both screenshots (`models.Message.Images`, new: each wire format's image block) and the markup and tree in `<untrusted>` blocks. It answers structured JSON: findings (a kind from a closed list such as `focus-visible`, `contrast`, and `target-size`, a Nielsen heuristic and 0–4 severity, the element, a summary, and a fix), the 10 heuristics' worst severities, the four personas (keyboard-only, screen-reader, low-vision, first-time), and `rebuild`. Consensus is per state and kind: the kind counts when ≥ 2 runs report it at severity ≥ 2 (severity is the lower median of those runs), and `rebuild` also needs 2 runs. A state with fewer than 2 answering runs fails the lane (exit 5). Lane B is impeccable 4.5.2 (`detect --json --no-config --no-advisory`, Playwright's Chromium through `CHROME_PATH`, telemetry and update checks off, pinned in `packages/floors` and `pnpm-lock.yaml`), axe (WCAG 2.2 AA tags), and two probes from the WP4.5 floor: a focus-indicator probe that tabs through the page and flags any control whose outline, shadow, border, background, colour, and underline are unchanged when focused, and controls under `[verify.floors.a11y] min_target_px` (44). Every lane B finding counts. Dispositions, worst first: `recapture` (a capture failed), `rebuild`, `fix` (any counted finding), `ship`. The combined disposition is the worse lane's, so lane A cannot override lane B (rule 7). `review` writes `.aayudha/review/<pr>/critique.json` with every run's reply, keeps the screenshots, and posts a replace-in-place `<!-- aayudha:review -->` PR comment; it exits 4 unless `ship`. `polish` needs that critique, re-runs both lanes, and reports each finding as resolved, remaining, or new (matched by lane, state, and kind); it exits 4 while any remain. The host makes the fixes (skill). `baseline [--sha]` refuses unless the checkout is at the SHA, captures each path in `[review] baseline` (new, proposed `["/"]`), and writes `baselines/<sha>/manifest.json`. Screenshots follow the proof media rules (D-6.5a). Tests: `TestLaneAConsensus` (2 of 3 counts, 1 of 3 and severity 1 do not, rebuild at 2 of 3), `TestLaneBAndDispositions`, `TestCompare`, and `TestWireFormats` (images per format) in CI `build`. `TestLaneBWithBrowser`, `TestReviewAndPolish` (CLI end to end with a scripted lane A), and `TestBaseline` run in CI `floors-web`. Live acceptance: `TestReviewLive` in the dispatch-only `ux-review-live.yml` (environment `ai-gateway`). It runs the real lane A and lane B on the planted and clean fixtures, then polish, where the build lane's model (`claude-opus-5-5` through the gateway) applies the critique to the page source with the polish skill as its instructions, standing in for the host as tier-2 evals do. Both lanes then re-run. Results: the run's artifact `ux-review-live-<run id>`
