# D-8.1a

## Plan assumption

WP5.5: a never-layer footprint ships only with the Engineering Manager's plan-time review on the ledger (`plan --approve`, human-only), whatever the streak; WP8.1 needs stories on L04, L07, and L09, which are `[autonomy] never` layers

## Finding

One human and no bot App (D1, D2). The agent drafts and checks every plan, so the one human approving it would be the only review, and an agent running `plan --approve` under the founder's account would record an approval that no one gave. In solo mode `plan --approve` records the one human as `as: solo`, which reads as a review. Founder decision (2026-10-12, relayed by the orchestrating thread): a never-layer story proceeds under a solo-mode bypass, recorded as a bypass and never as an approval

## Implemented instead

`aayu plan <n> --solo-bypass` (new registry input, human-only, refused for agents and when `--approve` is also passed): only in solo mode (no bot identity, no second paired human, the same checks as `ship --solo-bypass`), only for a plan on the ledger that needs a review and has none, and only after the human's own comment on the story starting "Solo-mode bypass:". It writes `em_review.bypass {as: "solo-bypass", reviewer: "none (solo mode)", recorded_by, recorded_at, comment_url, note}` to `specs/<n>/plan.json` and leaves `approved_by` and `as` empty. `ship` accepts a real approval or this bypass for a never layer; the ship record keeps it under `plan_review`, and the Complete section says "Plan-time review: none; … recorded a solo-mode plan-review bypass for layers …". `audit` lists these ships in their own section, "Solo-mode plan-review bypasses (`plan --solo-bypass`)", with their own count in the summary. Neither appears when there are none, so earlier reports replay unchanged. A plan already approved refuses the bypass. Tests: `TestPlan` (refusals, the bypass record, no approval claimed, and the bypass refused after an approval), `TestShipPassRules` (a bypass passes the plan check), `TestShipMobileWaiverAndSoloBypass` (ship record and Complete section), and `TestAuditPlanReviewBypass`. Every WP8.1 story shipped this way is listed in the WP8.1 report for the founder to review after the fact
