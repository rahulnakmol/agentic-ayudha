# D-5.6b

## Plan assumption

WP5.6 with WP5.1b: on demand, a stuck human gets a decision pack from the owning seat's twin: the question restated and checked, grounded facts, two or three options with trade-offs, a recommendation with its door, the cost of waiting, a Mentalist pre-mortem, the questions that would change the answer, and the one thing to decide; read-only and MCP-safe; nothing applied; no new verb

## Finding

The founder first asked (2026-10-15) for automatic blocker detection, a 4-hour decision pack, 24-hour resolution, new `[decisions]` keys, a tighter V8, and new status, retro, and audit columns, then withdrew it the same day: "I don't need automation. I just need a skill that helps me surface decision-making, coupled with a tight digital twin." None of the automation was built, and V8 (24 h escalate, 48 h provisional) is unchanged. `decide` was CLI and skill only, `ExposeNever`, and its positional was an issue, so a free question could not be passed and MCP could not reach it. Nothing chose a seat by topic

## Implemented instead

`decide` gains `--pack` (bool; pinned to `true` on MCP while `[mcp] expose_writes` is false, as `ready --check` is), `--seat <slug>`, and `--draft <file>`; its positional becomes a string that must be an issue unless `--pack` is set; it gains the MCP surface with `ExposeRead` (`--accept` stays human-only and never reaches MCP; `--pack` refuses `--accept` and `--waiver`). The verb count stays 32. `decide.OwningSeat` routes by topic words, narrower topics first (Auditor, Revenue Architect for growth metrics, Quality Architect, AI Native Engineer for incidents, DevOps, UX, Code Architect for design, Marketer, Mentalist, Product Strategist, Founder for direction, Engineering Manager for delivery and alignment), then by issue type, then to the Engineering Manager. `aayu decide <issue or "question"> --pack` reads the issue, its parent, up to ten linked items, open Decisions, and `status:blocked` items, wraps the issue body as untrusted data, and prints the owning twin's persona at the team stage and the pack outline; `--draft` checks a pack with `decide.CheckPackWith`: the eight `##` parts in order, a one-sentence question, two or three options (top-level items or `###` headings), a named door, `## Your decision` last and one paragraph, and the evidence rule, with citations resolved against the printed grounding or paths in the checkout (exit 4 on findings). The decide skill body says the owning twin speaks for the pack and the human records the outcome with `--accept`. Every persona's values include "helping a human decide quickly, and never letting a blocker sit" (`TestPersonas`). Eval case kind `[pack]` (routing, the recorded pack, and an incomplete pack that must fail); cases `decide/em-split-or-replan` (startup, with a pack missing its pre-mortem) and `decide/architect-tombstone-kind` (scaleup) on a shared synthetic grounding, both in tier 2 (the `personas` group, cap raised to 6 USD) with a rubric whose first item is "a busy human can decide from this in under five minutes"
