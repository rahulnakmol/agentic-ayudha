## Finding

Run 39045544983 (D-5.1w) found two faults in the retro skill, not in the cases or the rubric. First, the skill asked the artefact to end with "the change in one statement" but never said that the statement names a layer or a type and so needs its own citation. The em-startup answer cited its "Why" line under the change instead, and its revision only reworded the uncited line, so the floor failed. Second, an em-scaleup answer labelled a conclusion ("which suggests the gap was only visible at the L07 boundary") that its cited call path does not support; the skill required a conclusion to be labelled, not to follow from what it names.

## Implemented instead

`skills/retro/SKILL.md`: a labelled conclusion claims no more than the observations it names, so a reader of only those lines reaches it. When something became visible, why someone acted, and what would have happened otherwise are conclusions only when a record states them, and "only", "first", "never", or "all" is a claim of its own. The closing change carries its own citation in its sentence, since it names a layer, type, floor, or code. A finding of an uncited claim is fixed by citing that sentence or cutting it, never by rewording it. The rubric, the cases, and the floors are unchanged.
