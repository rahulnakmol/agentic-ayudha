## Implemented instead

The checks are fixed (`seats.CheckGroundingWith`): a citation with `<` or `>` is a template placeholder, needs no grounding, and is no evidence, and a unit whose only citations are placeholders is not a claim; a registry seat's `CHARTER.md` or `persona.toml` is always a known source; a unit that is only a bold label, or a lead-in of at most twelve words ending with a colon, is a heading and claims nothing; `seats.Words`, which bounds a startup answer (400) and a decision pack (500), counts body prose only (paragraphs and list items; headings, table rows, code, and comments excluded). `TestCheckGrounding` and `TestCheckStage` hold each fix with its other side (a real ungrounded path, a placeholder beside a real uncited claim, a non-seat charter, another file under a seat, a bold label followed by a claim, a long claim ending with a colon, body prose over the bound). The `personas` group is `gate = "advisory"` (as `brief-plain` is, D-7.3h): computed and printed, skipped by `evals-gate`; it becomes gating again when a run passes the bar. What remains after the fix is the model's quality: the judged runs scored 2–3 (claims stretched beyond their sources, packs long for a five-minute decision).
