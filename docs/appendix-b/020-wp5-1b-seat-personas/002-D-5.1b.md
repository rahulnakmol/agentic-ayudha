# D-5.1b

## Plan assumption

WP5.1b: one persona source rendered into charters, SKILL.md bodies, AGENTS.md, and the MCP prompt text; drift-checked; tier 1 on recorded outputs for the grounding rule with a planted uncited claim; one tier-2 EM-led case through `evals.yml` with a judge of another family; the same scenario at startup and enterprise must differ in depth while both stay grounded

## Finding

Skill bodies are hand-authored and another thread is rewriting `grill`'s; the generator owned only the frontmatter. The MCP server had prompts only for `grill` and `brief`, both built from the skill body. "Grounded" has no deterministic definition. `inspect` runs in the review lane, which is also the lane the tier-2 judge is routed through, so an `inspect` case would judge itself. The `skills` tier-2 group spent about 4.6 of its 6 USD cap. "Inferred by a sensible default" names no rule. `inspect`'s merge-gate model call does not read SKILL.md

## Implemented instead

`persona.toml` per seat, strictly decoded and validated at load (`seats.LoadPersonas`: every field, two or more values, habits, questions, and nevers, three or more practices, each with a source naming a year and an optional https URL, distinct stage artefacts none of which starts another, unique one-word names, and no company name or employment claim outside sources); `TestPersonas` also applies `design/brand.yaml`'s avoid list. The generator writes the Persona region of each charter, the AGENTS.md seat section (a Twin column plus each twin's purpose and voice, the stage rule, and the evidence rule), and a block between `<!-- BEGIN GENERATED: persona -->` and `<!-- END GENERATED: persona -->` placed after the body's title (replaced in place once present), so text outside the markers, including `grill`'s, stays hand-written. MCP: `grill` and `brief` prompts carry the block through the skill body, and a new `persona` prompt (seat, optional stage; default the repository's effective stage) renders the persona at a stage. `[team] stage` (enum) and `size`; unset, `Config.TeamStage` infers it from the size (≤ 15 startup, ≤ 150 scaleup, else enterprise), else from the paired seats plus the founder, so a solo pod is a startup. The evidence rule (`seats.CheckGrounding`): Markdown splits into paragraphs, list items, and table rows (header rows, headings, code fences, and comments excluded); a unit that matches a code, architecture, risk, or progress cue needs a citation (a path with optional line, a backticked directory, `#n`, `ADR-n`, a backticked `pkg.Symbol`, a backticked commit, `run <id>`, or a backticked config key); questions are exempt; a unit labelled `Analogy:` or `Anecdote:` needs a cited neighbour; an answer with statements but no citation fails; with a grounding text every citation must occur in it. `seats.CheckStage`: the practice's artefact for the stage is a heading prefix, no other stage's is, and a startup answer is at most 400 words. New eval case kind `[persona]`; tier 1 on `retro/em-startup`, `retro/em-enterprise`, `inspect/em-review` (scaleup; with `planted/review.md`, whose uncited claim must fail), `proof/qa-startup`, and `proof/qa-enterprise`, all on one synthetic grounding (`evals/cases/retro/grounding.md`, story #212 of a fixture repository, including an untrusted comment asking a bot to approve). Tier 2 only on the two `retro/` cases, in a new `personas` group (4 USD): the skill answers at the stage, gets the check findings once to revise, else scores 1 without the judge; the judge (`gpt-6.1-sol`, openai, against `retro`'s spec lane, anthropic) scores clarity, nudging, grounding, realism, stage fit, and guardrails. The names are proposals for the founder. `inspect`'s direct model prompt is unchanged (its merge-gate behaviour is out of scope)
