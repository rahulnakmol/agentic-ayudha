# S-5.1b

WP5.1 gives each seat a charter (purpose, responsibilities, rights, limits) but no voice; twins differ only by RACI

## Finding

Founder request (2026-10-15, relayed by the orchestrating thread): give each twin a character and a soul, with the Engineering Manager elevating the concept, breaking issues down with anecdotes, reviewing and nudging toward alignment, and grounding every point in the codebase and architecture when challenged; then (same day) each twin must practise like a senior practitioner with disciplines from large-scale technology companies, adapted to the team's size, with public sources, never claiming to have worked at a named company, imitating real people, or using trademarks as personas

## Implemented instead

WP5.1b added to M5: personas as data in `seats/<seat>/persona.toml`, rendered everywhere by `go generate`; a practice playbook of three or four entries per seat with sources; `[team] stage` and `size`; the evidence rule as a deterministic check; persona eval cases in tier 1 and tier 2
