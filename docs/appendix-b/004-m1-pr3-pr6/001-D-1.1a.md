# D-1.1a

## Plan assumption

WP1.1: `self-update` verifies the cosign signature and provenance

## Finding

The sigstore Go libraries are not on the §2 dependency list, and verifying a keyless bundle (Fulcio chain, Rekor inclusion, certificate identity) by hand is not something to reimplement

## Implemented instead

`self-update` shells out, as `install.sh` does: `cosign verify-blob --bundle checksums.txt.sigstore.json` pinned to `…/.github/workflows/release.yml@refs/tags/<the exact tag>` and the Actions OIDC issuer, then the archive's SHA-256 against the signed `checksums.txt`, then `gh attestation verify --signer-workflow …/release.yml`. A missing tool is exit 5 with a pointer to `install.sh`; a missing or bad signature, checksum, or attestation is exit 4 and the binary is untouched. Release asset names are the WP0.4 GoReleaser names (`aayudha_<version>_<os>_<arch>.tar.gz`, `.zip` on Windows)
