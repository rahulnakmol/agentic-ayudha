# D-1.2a

## Plan assumption

WP1.2 accept: "a 24 h SLA started Friday 17:00 London time expires the following Monday 16:00"

## Finding

With the `[time]` default of Mon–Fri 09:00–18:00 that instant is 8 business hours away (Friday 17–18 plus Monday 09–16). 24 business hours land on Wednesday 14:00. No consistent reading of "business hours" gives Monday 16:00 for 24 h

## Implemented instead

`[decisions] sla_hours` counts business hours literally (`config.Clock.Add`, local wall-clock windows in the IANA zone, so DST never shifts them). The table test pins both: 8 h from Friday 17:00 → Monday 16:00, and 24 h → Wednesday 14:00, each also across the 25 October 2026 clock change (16:00 and 14:00 local, now GMT)
