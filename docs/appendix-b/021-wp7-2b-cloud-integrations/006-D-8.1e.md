# D-8.1e

## Plan assumption

WP8.1: the example is measured "from `grill goal` to `release`"; WP7.2 `measure` writes the Goal's Result from exports; WP7.8 `release` stamps stories released and schedules `measure` 28 days later; V3: synthetic data only

## Finding

No real employees or analytics exist, and the orchestrator owns this repository's promotions, so `release` runs after this WP reports and its 28-day `measure` due date is in the future

## Implemented instead

Both exports are synthetic and reproducible from code: `examples/benefits-qa/analytics/simulate` drives 120 fictional employees per run (seeded) through the HR-ticket channel (baseline, 2026-08-10 to 2026-09-07) and through the product's real ask workflow (2026-09-14 to 2026-10-12); both files are committed and `TestExportsReproducible` regenerates them byte for byte. Goal #125 and Initiative #126 are measured on the product-channel export before the rolling snapshot, so their Result is the simulated outcome of the merged code, not a post-release window; the release's own `measure/due` record stays for a later run. When #133's rebuild left out two HR answers that fell after the baseline window, the export's SHA-256 changed but not the measured baseline (31 of 120, 0.258, since `measure` reads the half-open window); the Goal and Initiative bodies were updated to the new hashes and re-snapshotted with `aayu ready`. The Release issue #144 includes Features #127–#129 for the orchestrator's `aayu release`
