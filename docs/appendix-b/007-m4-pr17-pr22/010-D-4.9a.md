# D-4.9a

## Plan assumption

WP4.9 (0.1 part): a story whose footprint includes `platforms: ios` gets `skipped: mobile`; `ship` on it exits 4; with a linked floor-waiver Decision `ship` proceeds and the pack records the waiver

## Finding

The schema has no per-story platforms field, `decide --waiver` is WP5.6, and `ship` is WP4.10 (PR22)

## Implemented instead

The `mobile` floor applies when `[verify] platforms` lists `ios` or `android` and records `skipped: mobile`, which `proof.BlocksShip` treats as not holding (unless `[verify.floors.mobile] skipped_blocks_ship = false`). A floor-waiver Decision is a `type:decision` sub-issue of the story whose Links say `waives #<story>` and whose title or body names the floor; `verify` marks the check `waived` with `waiver: "#<n>"`, so the pack records it and the floors hold. Accepting a waiver by its `[decisions] authority` is n/a until WP5.6. `ship` refusing a non-holding pack (exit 4) and proceeding on a waived one is tested with `ship` in PR22
