# D-4.3r

## Plan assumption

S-4.3r; P7 of the research: `[verify.ratchets.<name>]` with a command, a direction, a noise margin, and a committed ceiling; a new ratchet stays advisory until reruns show its noise is below the margin

## Finding

CONSTRAINTS.md's coverage floor (`min_diff_percent`) is per PR and depends on the size of each diff, so it has no "better value" to hold. The zero-count floors (SAST, secrets, deps, a11y) are already at their tightest. The performance budgets apply only to stories with web criteria. A CI job cannot push to `dev`, and a bot PR per tightening is the review load the research rejected. The coverage of the module's `go test ./...` is measured on every push. Local and CI runs can differ slightly (toolchain, tools installed)

## Implemented instead

One ratchet, `[coverage]`: the statement coverage of `go test -coverprofile=coverage.out ./...` across the module (blocks counted once, as `go tool cover -func` reports), with `floor`, `margin` (0.5 points), and an optional `waiver`. `go run ./internal/ratchet/cmd/ratchet -coverprofile coverage.out [-base <sha>]` checks three rules. (1) Coverage below `floor − margin` fails, on PRs and pushes. (2) On a PR, coverage at `floor + 2 × margin` or more fails until the PR tightens the floor with `-update`, which sets it to coverage less the margin, rounded down to a tenth, and never lowers it. That is the "automatic" tightening: the tool computes it and CI requires it, so no daily job and no extra PR are needed. A push to `dev` does not apply this rule, so two PRs that each raised coverage a little do not turn `dev` red; the next PR tightens. (3) A floor lower than the base's fails unless `waiver = <n>` names a Decision that the base did not already name, and that the job reads from GitHub as an accepted floor waiver for coverage: labelled `decision:waiver`, titled "Waive the coverage floor on #…", closed as completed by a person, with "waive, accepted by @" in its Decision section (`aayu decide --waiver coverage <story-or-bug>`, then `--accept waive`; the rule `verify` applies, WP5.6). A base without `ratchets.toml` has floor 0. The `build` job gains `issues: read`. The proxy check (noise against the margin): the same tree measured 63.1 % in the orb (Go 1.27) and 63.3 % on the CI runner (Go 1.25, run 38833496218), inside the margin; the initial floor, 62.8 %, is `-update` on the CI value. AGENTS.md and CONTRIBUTING.md say how to tighten. Tests: `TestCheck` (the margin on both sides of the floor, a stale floor on a PR but not on a push, tightening, a loosening without a waiver, with the base's waiver, and with a new one), `TestTotal` (a block in two profile lines counts once), `TestAcceptedWaiver` (only an accepted coverage waiver closed by a person)
