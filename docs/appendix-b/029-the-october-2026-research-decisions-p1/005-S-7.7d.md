# S-7.7d

## Plan assumption

WP7.6, WP7.7, S-7.7c: a correction becomes retro Feedback, and evolve fixes it by rewriting a skill with an eval case, so every correction lands in the prose layer

## Finding

Founder decision (2026-10-17, relayed by the orchestrating thread), from the "inverse pyramid" for correcting agents: most effort goes into the codebase (types, invariants, boundaries), then static analysis (lint rules, tests, CI checks, verify), then prose (skills, AGENTS.md, rules) only for what needs judgement, and least into human review. Each correction is first classified by the highest layer that can fix it for good, with the reason; codebase and static-check corrections become a Story or Bug whose proof is the new type, invariant, lint rule, or test; only prose corrections go through the eval-first skill rewrite; review corrections change what the environment surfaces for review. The classification is visible on the Feedback issue and counted per layer in retro's report, and evolve refuses a skill rewrite for a correction classified above prose unless the reason says why code or a check cannot catch it. The principle was not in ETHOS.md

## Implemented instead

ETHOS 10, "Fix the system before the prose". `aayu retro --classify <feedback> --layer codebase\|check\|prose\|review --reason …` records the layer; codebase, check, and review corrections file a Story; evolve takes only prose Feedback; and every retro report gains a "Fix layer of Feedback" table. The deterministic parts (the record's completeness, the Story, the label, the counts, evolve's refusal) are code; the layer itself is the twin's judgement with its recorded reason. The parallel transcript-mining thread's proposals enter here: one `retro --classify` per correction
