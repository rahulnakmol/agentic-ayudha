# D-7.7e

## Plan assumption

S-7.7d and D-7.7d: Feedback filed before the classification existed stayed unclassified, and evolve's refusal was the only place that said so; the fix Story carried no layer, so fix work was not filterable and retro could not count it

## Finding

Founder follow-ups on #323 (2026-10-17, relayed by the orchestrating thread): old Feedback must not sit stuck; classify #190 and #152 with a real reason (or close #190 with evidence if the personas work fixed it), reopen any Feedback closed without a fix, make unclassified open Feedback surface on its own (a doctor row and the session-start summary, with the classify command); and give the fix Story its Feedback's `fix:<layer>` label, a label rather than a new work-item type, so retro counts fix Stories shipped per layer. Host hooks make no network call (the session-start and status-line hooks read the last home screen)

## Implemented instead

**Surfacing:** `aayu doctor` gains a `feedback-layers` row that warns with each open Feedback issue without a `fix:<layer>` label and the command (`workitems.ClassifyCommand`); the home screen (`aayu`, `aayu next`) lists them in the same words (`journey.UnclassifiedNote`, `unclassified_feedback` in `--json`); the status line counts "Feedback to classify"; the session-start hook prints the note from the last home screen, with its age past ten minutes (`Env.sessionNotes`, passed to `hosts.RunHook`; Cursor gets it as `additional_context`). The hook stays offline, so it is as fresh as the last `aayu` or `aayu next`. **Label:** the Story `retro --classify` files carries `fix:<layer>`; rerunning the command adds it to a Story filed earlier, swaps it on a reclassification, and removes it when the correction moves to `prose`, which no Story fixes. A Feedback titled "Feedback: …" by the issue form no longer repeats the prefix in its Story's title. **retro:** the "Fix layer of Feedback" table adds "Fix Stories" (Stories carrying the layer's label, opened before the cursor) and "Shipped" (those merged into dev, or done, by the cursor). **The old Feedback:** no closed Feedback exists (`gh issue list --state closed --label type:feedback` is empty), so none was reopened. #190 and #152 are classified `prose` with their reasons on the issues: no type or test can decide whether a cited sentence claims more than its source (#190) or whether an everyday restatement keeps a fact (#152), and the decidable parts are already checks (`seats.CheckGroundingWith`, D-5.1g.a; `brief` `absenceClaims`, D-7.3e). #190 is not closed, because D-5.1h to D-5.1j still find the defect and the personas group is below 4.0. Tests: `TestChecksGolden` (an unclassified Feedback and one with an unknown `fix:` label are listed; a classified one and a pull request are not), `TestHomeModes` (the home screen in every mode), `TestPluginHooksAndStatusLine` (no note with nothing to classify; the note at session start for Claude Code and Cursor, and the status-line count), `TestRetroClassify` (the Story's label, its swap, its removal at prose, the title), and `TestRetroFixLayers` (fix Stories and shipped per layer, with a Story merged after the cursor and one opened after it). The regression test is `TestRetroClassify`, which fails on the `dev` before this change
