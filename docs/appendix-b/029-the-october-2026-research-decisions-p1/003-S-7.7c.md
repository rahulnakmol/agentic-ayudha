# S-7.7c

## Plan assumption

WP7.7 and D-7.7a: `evolve` takes one accepted retro Feedback about a skill, quarterly; eval-first gate

## Finding

Founder decision 2 (P4, research Q3): evolve proposes a skill change only when at least two accepted Feedback issues point at the same skill, at most once a month per skill; it rewrites the guidance rather than appending rules; and a recurrence check reports whether a fixed problem came back

## Implemented instead

Three rules in `evolve` and one in `retro`, all deterministic: the two-Feedback rule and the monthly cap in the verb, the rewrite rule in the eval-first gate (so CI enforces it), and the recurrence check in the retro report. The `evolve` loop moves from quarterly to monthly (day 2, 10:00 in `[time] tz`; it only lists). The skill body is rewritten to match: name the owning surface (missing, wrong, or unclear instruction), stop when the Feedback issues do not share a cause, and rewrite the section as a whole
