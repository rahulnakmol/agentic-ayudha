# D-7.7d

## Plan assumption

S-7.7d: "filed through aayu, with a Completion Goal whose proof is the new type, invariant, lint rule or test"; "a Story or Bug"; the classification visible on the Feedback issue

## Finding

A Bug needs reproduction steps and an observed failure that a classification does not have. A Completion Goal criterion needs an evidence kind (`screenshot`, `recording`, `sr-transcript`, `api-trace`, `metric`), and none is "a test fails"; the proof is named in the expected result. Retro already fetches every issue's labels, but not their comments, and a snapshot that grew comment bodies would grow every report. GitHub has no structured field on an issue; aayu keeps state in replace-in-place comments (inspect, respond)

## Implemented instead

**Record:** the `fix:codebase`, `fix:check`, `fix:prose`, and `fix:review` labels (created by `init`; `workitems.FixLayerOf`) plus one comment marked `<!-- aayudha:fix-layer -->`, upserted, with the reason, the not-catchable reason, the Story, and a JSON state line. `--reason` is always required. `--not-catchable` (why no type, invariant, lint rule, or test can catch it) is required for `prose` and `review` and optional above. `--criterion` is required for `codebase`, `check`, and `review`, refused for `prose`, parsed with `workitems.ParseCriteria`, and for `codebase` and `check` must name the proof in backticks. Refusals exit 2 before any write. A reclassification swaps the label, rewrites the comment, and never files a second Story. **Story, not Bug:** the filed item is always a Story (`type:story`, `status:backlog`, bot-tagged), titled "Fix at the `<layer>` layer (Feedback #n): …", with the reason in its Summary and the criterion as its Completion Goal; `grill story` completes the rest of Ready, and a person who prefers a Bug files one and reclassifies. **evolve:** `acceptedFeedback` reads each accepted Feedback's layer from its labels and, above prose, its comment state; `evolveRefusal` refuses an unclassified Feedback, a `review` one, and a `codebase` or `check` one without `--not-catchable` (exit 4). Only Feedback it accepts counts toward the two-issue rule of S-7.7c, and `aayu evolve` with no flags shows "not for evolve: …" beside the rest. **retro:** "Fix layer of Feedback" counts every Feedback issue opened before the cursor by its label at the cursor, `unclassified` included, from the labels the snapshot already holds, so `--replay` stays byte-identical. A report from before this change replays with the binary that wrote it. Every Feedback body retro files now says to classify it first. Skills: retro gains step 3, the order of questions with stop at the first yes; evolve says a rewrite is the third layer and to reclassify rather than rewrite when code or a check could catch it. Docs: ETHOS 10, how-evals ("Fix layer first"), guide-learn, journey-8, when-to-use-what. Tests: `TestRetroClassify` (an incomplete record writes nothing; a check correction files a Story whose criterion names the test; reclassifying to prose swaps the label, keeps one comment, files no second Story), `TestEvolveVerb` (unclassified and codebase-without-reason Feedback are refused and do not count; with a not-catchable reason the codebase one counts), and `TestRetroFixLayers` (counts by layer, PRs, other types, and later Feedback excluded). The regression test is `TestEvolveVerb`, which fails on the evolve of `dev` before this change
