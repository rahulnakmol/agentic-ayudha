# D-4.3s

## Plan assumption

S-4.3r and Story #400 (Feedback #327, research P1 in docs/research/agent-steering-patterns-2026-10.md): the skill-eval bars in `evals/eval.toml` and the cases' rubrics had no ratchet, and agents lowered them to pass an eval 13 times in 9 threads, each caught only by a reviewer

## Finding

Whether a change lowers a bar is a property of the diff; only the decision to lower one needs a person, and a floor waiver already records that. #331, filed by `retro --classify`, could not pass Ready (no Measure, Threshold, Scenario, Environment, or effort) and no `aayu` verb edits an issue body, so #400 replaces it with the same Completion Goal

## Implemented instead

`evals.CheckBars` compares `evals/` with the PR's base and lists every lowering: `pass_score`, `min_pass_k`, or `min_mean` lower or `max_drop` higher; a case moved into a group whose `gate` or `pass_k` is advisory; a case removed; a case's deterministic rule (case.toml scope, session.toml require, forbid, say_forbid) removed or its `pattern` or `allow` changed; a rubric item (criterion, anchor, or paragraph, joined across wrapped lines) dropped or reworded so that it loses any word (an item that only gains words is kept). A lowering fails CI's `build` job (the coverage ratchet's command, given `-base`) unless `evals/eval.toml` names a new `bar_waiver`: a floor waiver for the new `eval-bar` floor (`decide.Waivable`) that a human accepted (`ratchet.AcceptedWaiver`); one named on the base does not count again. Raising a bar and adding a case, a group, a rule, or rubric words need nothing, so a new group still starts advisory. `TestEvalBarRatchet` proves each kind of lowering fails with no waiver, passes with a new accepted one, fails with an unaccepted or reused one, and that the repository's own evals load and lower nothing against themselves. A pattern change that only tightens a rule still counts as a change: the check cannot tell tightening from loosening for a regular expression, so it fails closed
