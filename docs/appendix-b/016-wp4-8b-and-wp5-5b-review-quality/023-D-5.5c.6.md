# D-5.5c.6

## Plan assumption

WP5.5c: every hand-off gets a fresh worktree (local) or branch (cloud); the receiver breaks the work down, uses its own subagents, verifies the Completion Goal with evidence, and returns what it proved; the calling host reports it

## Finding

A re-hand-off after a failure reused `.aayudha/worktrees/<n>-<agent>` and the story branch. Receivers report in different places (a PR body, a CLI's final message)

## Implemented instead

`delegate/<n>/state.json` counts hand-offs. Hand-off *k* > 1 uses `story/<n>-<slug>-<k>` and `.aayudha/worktrees/<n>-<agent>-<k>`, and a local hand-off skips a number an old worktree or remote branch still holds; syncs keep their hand-off's branch and worktree. The brief adds "How to work and report": break the work down by checkpoint, use your own subagents, verify every criterion with evidence before you report, and write a `## What I proved` section (PR body in the cloud, final message locally), with the line that the merge gate's proof decides. `build --delegate` reads that section from the PR body or the CLI's output, keeps it on the ledger, reports it as the receiver's preview (`delegation.proved`), and quotes it in the PR body aayu opens. It is untrusted text, never a gate
