# D-5.5b.1

## Plan assumption

The request names `aayu delegate <story> --to <agent>` and prefers extending `build` if that keeps the registry clean

## Finding

Delegation needs exactly `build`'s gates (Ready and body hash, lease, budget, halt), its worktree, branch, commit, and PR rules, its checkpoint record, and its RACI row (AI Native Engineer). A `delegate` verb would duplicate all of them and add a 33rd verb

## Implemented instead

`build` gains `--delegate <codex\|cursor\|claude\|amp\|auto>` and `--local`; the slash commands give the short form. No new verb
