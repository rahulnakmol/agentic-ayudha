# D-5.3b.b

## Plan assumption

WP5.3b: nothing enters the spec unless the human confirmed it; record confirmations so it is auditable which criteria came from whom

## Finding

A work item has no field for open questions, parked ideas, or sources, and an unknown section fails Parse. The binary cannot see the conversation, so it cannot verify a confirmation it was not shown

## Implemented instead

Story and Bug gain four optional Ready fields (schema v1, additive, so older bodies still parse): Assumptions, Open questions (`answered:` or `deferred:` per line), Parked ideas (`<name>: …`, out of scope), and Provenance: `Request: "<the human's words>"`, `Vague: "<qualifier>" → criteria N, M` or `→ deferred: <why>`, and one `N. stated\|confirmed\|default (<dimension>): "<the human's words>"; facts: <id>, …` per criterion. The issue forms regenerate (and this repository's copies in `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/` are refreshed). `grill.Check` (grill and `ready`): when Provenance is present, every criterion has exactly one source with a quote, every qualifier of the Request is resolved to existing criteria or deferred with a `deferred:` open question, Assumptions is not empty, every open question is answered or deferred, and no parked idea's name appears in the Completion Goal. `grill --idea` requires Provenance, its Request must quote the idea, and every cited fact must be in the checkout's assessment. Whether the human really said the quoted words is checked where the conversation is visible: in the evals (D-5.3b.d); in a host the readback the human confirms is the check, and the issue body (hashed at Ready) is the audit record. Case scope rules no longer read the spec record (they guard criteria; the record is where unconfirmed ideas belong). The guard (`ready --guard`) and the Ready checks inside `build`, `verify`, `plan`, and `release` do not apply the new rules, so Ready already granted is never withdrawn by them
