# D-5.3b.d

## Plan assumption

WP5.3b evals: a bakery "high-quality UX" case on a synthetic repository fixture and a non-UX case; tier 1 deterministic; tier 2 rubric on question quality, concreteness, and fidelity with a scripted human; group `skills`

## Finding

WP3.4's tier 2 is one skill reply per run. A scripted human cannot follow free-form questions by their wording. The `skills` group's 6 USD cap and the job's 60 minutes fit single replies only

## Implemented instead

Fixture `evals/cases/grill/testdata/bakery` (a never-built Next.js ordering app: a five-screen `/order` flow whose pay screen has no error state, `/menu/search` over a 40-product seed, tokens, components, events, an e2e test, LAYERS.md, Lighthouse budgets, no k6 script). Cases `grill/bakery-ux` and `grill/search-fast` record the conversation (`output/transcript.json`) and replay `grill story --assess`, `grill story --idea … --create`, and `ready --check` in a copy of the fixture (new case keys `repo`, `transcript`, `cite`; `[tier2] human`). The scripted human (`human.toml`) answers by the catalogue dimension the skill tags each question with (`confirm`, `default`, `park`, or `defer`); any other topic gets a fallback that parks it, so an unscripted idea can never become a requirement. Tier 2 runs the skill turn by turn (JSON turns: `say`, up to five tagged `questions`, `done`, `draft`) with the request and the fixture's assessment as untrusted data; after `max_rounds` the human asks for the spec and a skill still asking scores 1. Deterministic floors before the judge (rule 7), shared with tier 1 (`evals.Concrete`): Ready, `grill.Check`, cited facts exist, at least `cite` criteria cite one, the Request is the human's, every criterion's quote occurs in the request or a reply, its dimension was answered `confirm` or `default` (a `default` source only where the human accepted one), and the case's scope rules. The judge (gpt-6.1-sol; the skill is claude-opus-5-5) sees the whole conversation with each reply's kind and the spec. Results keep each conversation run's exchanges and final criteria (`transcript`). `skills` `max_usd` 6 → 20 and the tier-2 job's timeout 60 → 90 minutes. TestTier1GrillConversation (each defect class fails with its reason), TestTier2GrillConversation (turns, answers, untrusted wrapping, a parked topic scored 1 without the judge, a skill that never finishes)
