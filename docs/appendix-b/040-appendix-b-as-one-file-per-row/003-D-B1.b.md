# D-B1.b

## Plan assumption

A Story that `aayu retro --classify` files goes through `aayu ready` and `aayu build` unchanged.

## Finding

`retro --classify` files a Story with only a Summary and a Completion Goal, and Ready also needs Measure, Threshold, Scenario, Environment, and an Effort label. No verb edits an existing issue body (`grill` creates; `triage` never edits bodies), so completing the Story needs a person's hand edit.

## Implemented instead

The completed spec was filed with `aayu grill story --create` as Story 397, which replaces Story 334, then made Ready and built through `aayu build`. Story 334 stays open until a person closes it. A follow-up could let `retro --classify` take the Ready fields, or `grill` complete an existing Story.
