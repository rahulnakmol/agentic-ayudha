# D-5.3b.h

## Plan assumption

WP5.3b and D-5.3b.g: placement checks refuse a criterion whose place word is not in its quoted human words; WP3.4: tier 2 runs nightly, after relevant merges to `dev`, on promotion, and on dispatch

## Finding

Run 38692692002 scored search-fast 4.8 and bakery-ux 2.8: both 1s were placement refusals of criteria that said "on that screen", "on the same screen", or "on the review screen" without quoting a reply naming it. The founder (2026-10-15) kept `grill.Check` strict and asked the skill to quote the reply instead, and asked that the advisory `conversations` and `personas` groups (about 13 and 20 USD a run) stop running nightly: on demand, and on pushes to `dev` or pull requests that change grill or persona files. Tier 2 needs the `ai-gateway` environment, which only `dev`, `rolling`, and `main` may use, so it cannot run on a pull request

## Implemented instead

Skill only: `skills/grill/SKILL.md` (outside the persona block) gains "Placement quotes": a criterion that says where something is quotes every human reply naming that place, even one given to another question (`"… on the same screen" / "… on the review screen"`), names the place instead of "that screen" or "this page", and leaves an unconfirmed place out with a `deferred:` open question; `references/story.md` and `bug.md` say the same. `TestCheck` adds a criterion whose placements come from two quoted replies (accepted) and one that says "on that screen" (refused). `conversations` and `personas` get `events = ["workflow_dispatch", "push"]`, so the schedule never runs them; `evals.yml` runs them on a push only to `dev` and only when the push changes their files: for conversations `skills/grill/`, `evals/cases/grill/`, `internal/grill/`, `internal/evals/conversation.go`; for personas `seats/*/persona.toml`, `internal/seats/persona.go` (it renders the persona blocks), `evals/cases/retro/` and `decide/`, `internal/evals/persona.go`. Pushes to `rolling` and `main` skip them too (neither gates). A pull request gets tier 1 in `go test`; its tier 2 runs on the push to `dev` that merges it. `brief`, `brief-plain`, and `skills` keep their schedule. Test: TestGroups (events of both groups)
