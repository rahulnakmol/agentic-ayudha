# D-7.3c

## Plan assumption

WP7.3: the grounding footer and the adversarial pass hold a brief to its sources

## Finding

After the D-7.3b fix round the remaining tier-2 marks were for evidence claims the grounding could not trace: the packs in the grounding listed artifacts but not which criterion each proves, nor inspect's verdicts. Founder decision, 2026-10-12: add the grounding, do not weaken the claims, and control cost

## Implemented instead

Each grounding pack carries its criterion → evidence map: every criterion of the story's Ready snapshot on the ledger (id, evidence kind, "state: …; expected: …"; the pack's own criterion ids when there is no snapshot) with the artifacts the pack maps to it (path, kind, SHA-256; empty when unmapped), plus the inspect disposition (model, head, who posted it) and per-criterion verdict and judgement from inspect's replace-in-place comment on the pack's PR, only when that result is for the pack's head. A mapped criterion is citable as `[P1.c3]`; an unmapped one has no citation. The evidence rule (`evidence` check, deterministic, before any model call): a sentence that says a criterion is proved, met, shown, verified, or evidenced, or that a recording, trace, screenshot, or transcript shows something, needs the criterion's map entry cited in the same paragraph or list item; a criterion with no mapped evidence must be said to be not proved; negated sentences claim nothing. The footer lists each pack's map under it (evidence with hashes, "No evidence in the pack; not proved", inspect verdicts), and citations of an entry link to it. New tier-1 case `brief/unmapped-criterion` on `grounding-unmapped.json` (criterion 3 unmapped, inspect `fix`): its draft passes, and `TestTier1UnmappedCriterionClaim` plants a proof claim and a citation of the missing entry, each failing. Cost: `eval -skip <prefixes>` leaves tier-2 cases out (recorded as `skipped` in the results file); `evals.yml` passes `-skip brief/` on pushes to `dev`, so the brief cases (about 6–7 USD a run) run nightly, on dispatch, and on pushes to `rolling` and `main`, whose results the promotion gate reads, while the stub keeps its trigger; a push touching only `internal/brief/` no longer starts tier 2. `max_usd` stays 12.0
