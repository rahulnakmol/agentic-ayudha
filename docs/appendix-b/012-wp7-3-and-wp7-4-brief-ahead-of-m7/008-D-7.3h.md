# D-7.3h

## Plan assumption

WP7.3: register evals; D-7.3g gated the brief group on the mean

## Finding

The rolling candidate's tier 2 (evals.yml run 38230815198 on 662cd99) failed the brief group even on the mean: eli5 3.20 and ste 3.00 (engineer-ideate 4.00, regulatory-close 5.00). The judge marked eli5 down mostly for everyday restatements ("away from the screen", "they like to check offline"), which the eli5 register asks for, so its rubric is in tension with itself. It marked ste down for "did not run" and "cannot run", which the `absence` check deliberately allows when a floor detail says it. One ste run was failed before the judge by the `unit` check on "saves" in a Limits-style sentence, "the evidence does not show that the export saves time": a false positive, since "saves" there is a verb. Founder decision, 2026-10-12

## Implemented instead

`[[groups]]` take `gate = "enforced"` (default) or `"advisory"`; any other value is refused. An advisory-gate group's tier 2 is computed and printed as before, but `eval -compare` and `-tier 2` exit 0 on a failing result (printing that the group does not gate), and `evals-gate` compares only gating groups (`eval -groups -gating`). New group `brief-plain` (prefixes `brief/eli5-`, `brief/ste-`, 6 USD, pass_k and gate advisory) holds the plain registers, before `brief`, which keeps engineer-ideate and regulatory-close on the D-7.3g mean bar at 6 USD. `evals.yml` skips both brief groups on pushes to `dev`. Tests: `TestGroupGatePolicy` (brief-plain does not gate; brief, skills, and an unknown group do; an unknown gate value is refused), `TestCompareAdvisoryGate` (the run's means exit 0 in brief-plain and fail in brief), and `TestGroups` for `-groups -gating`. The `unit` check counts "saves" only as a counted noun (after a number, "count(s)", "number of", or "many"), with fixtures on both sides, including the run's sentence. Feedback [#152](https://github.com/rahulnakmol/agentic-ayudha/issues/152), filed through `internal/actions`, describes the eli5 restatement versus fact-fidelity tension and the ste absence-phrasing conflict for `evolve`. #64 and #65 stay open on the tier-2 criterion
