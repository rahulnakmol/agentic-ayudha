# D-7.3a

## Plan assumption

WP7.3: registers `eli5\|ste\|engineer\|regulatory`; purposes `explain\|ideate\|innovate\|close`; HTML artifact with grounding footer; STE100 linter; a11y check on the HTML; `close` refuses without an authoritative pack and Result; ground rule 5: the `brief` adversarial pass calls a model directly. Accept: planted 30-word sentence fails STE lint; zero serious/critical axe findings and every image has alt text; `brief close` on a story without an authoritative pack exits 4

## Finding

Built ahead of M7 (founder scheduling, 2026-10-12). The registry declares `brief` hybrid (the host's model writes), but the plan names no draft format, grounding format, or citation syntax. The ASD-STE100 dictionary is ASD's copyright and cannot be redistributed, and no deterministic part-of-speech tagger is available. A Story or Bug has no Result field (its Complete section has Outcome). The plan does not say which packs a `close` on a Goal or Feature needs, how a pack read from the ledger proves it is authoritative, or the exit codes of a lint miss and an unsupported claim

## Implemented instead

`internal/brief` and `aayu brief <issue> [--register] [--purpose] [--draft <file>]` (new registry input `draft`; defaults `engineer`, `explain`). Without `--draft` it prints the grounding to write from (`--json` for the struct): the subject with its parsed fields and body SHA-256 (untrusted, wrapped in a provenance block), every work item under it, the best pack of each story and bug from the ledger (`P1`…: authority, merge-gate run, head, floors, artifacts, SHA-256 of `pack.json`), the Result (`R`; a Story's or Bug's Outcome), and the measure snapshots the Result names (`D1`…, found under `.aayudha/state/measure/`). The host writes a Markdown draft: `# Title`, the takeaway paragraph, sections, and citations `[#n]`, `[P1]`, `[R]`, `[D1]`. With `--draft`, deterministic checks first (rule 7), exit 3 with no model call on any miss: structure (title, takeaway first, no raw HTML or Markdown images), every citation names a source and at least one exists, purpose (`ideate` an `## Ideas` section with two or more items, `innovate` a `## Hypothesis` section, `close` cites `R` and an authoritative pack), and the register: `eli5` a Flesch-Kincaid grade of at most 6 (0.39 × words/sentences + 11.8 × syllables/words − 15.59, vowel-group syllables, code spans and numbers one syllable); `ste` the STE lint: sentences of at most 20 words (colons and semicolons do not end one), every word on `skills/brief/references/ste-words.md` (aayudha's own list, 562 words by part of speech, regular `-s/-es/-ies/-ed` forms pass, plus 26 words to avoid with their replacements) unless it is a technical name (a code span, a number, an acronym, a capitalised word inside a sentence, or a word of the titles of the subject and its items or a pack's floor names), and one instruction per sentence (clauses split at commas, semicolons, `and`, `or`, `then`; a sentence that opens with an approved base-form verb, or a lead-in such as "If …," and then one, gives as many instructions as its clauses that open with one); `regulatory` a citation on every paragraph and list item. Then the adversarial pass: one structured call on the review lane (the direct host's default when the host's own is host-managed) with the grounding and the draft as untrusted blocks, refused unless its family differs from the spec lane's writer (`models.CheckFamilies`); any claim the sources do not support is exit 4 and nothing is rendered; `--dry-run` skips the call and the page says so. The page is one self-contained HTML file (goldmark with raw HTML off; Content Security Policy `default-src 'none'`, inline styles and script, `data:` images): a header with the register, purpose, and pack counts; citations linked to a grounding footer that lists every source with its hashes, the checks that passed, the adversarial verdict, and the accessibility result; written to `.aayudha/state/briefs/<n>/<register>-<purpose>.html` with a `.json` manifest. Accessibility: every `<img>` has alt text and every SVG image a title (checked in Go), then `verify.CheckPage` runs the WP4.5 web floor script on the file (axe-core with the WCAG 2.2 AA tags, Tab reachability, reduced motion); a serious or critical finding or a missing alt text is exit 3 with the page kept for review, and missing Playwright or axe-core is exit 5. `close` is refused (exit 4) before anything else unless the subject has a Result and every story and bug it covers (the subject itself, or every one under it, at least one) has an authoritative pack whose floors hold; a pack counts as authoritative only when it sits on an `…/authoritative/` ledger path and records `aayudha-merge-gate.yml` as its workflow. Tests: `TestSTELint` (the planted 30-word sentence, 20 and 21 words, an unapproved word with its replacement, two instructions), `TestReadability` (the formula on a hand-worked sentence), `TestCheckRules`, `TestBriefCLI` (close without an authoritative pack exits 4; a planted 30-word sentence exits 3 before any model call; an unsupported claim exits 4; a serious axe finding exits 3; close renders once the merge gate's pack and an Outcome exist), and `TestBriefPageWithBrowser` in the `floors` workflow (`floors-web`: 0 serious and 0 critical with Playwright 1.64.0 and axe-core 4.14.0)
