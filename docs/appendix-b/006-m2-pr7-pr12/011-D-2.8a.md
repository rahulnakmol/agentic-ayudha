# D-2.8a

## Plan assumption

WP2.8: rollups with proof coverage, streak, blocked/over-budget, shipped-but-unmeasured, decisions near SLA, provisional decisions, cost; outputs table, `--json`, Goal comment, `--digest`, MCP resource; `status --all`; parent-close rule

## Finding

"Near SLA", "over budget", and the cost source are not defined; `status` is a read verb exposed over MCP, yet the Goal comment and the parent-close rule write. Measure is WP7.2

## Implemented instead

Rollups count every work item under a Goal, Initiative, or Feature at any depth (sub-issue API, every page): by Delivery Status, health, and risk; blocked (`status:blocked`); over budget (the item's `cost_usd` in the local actions log above `[budget] per_story_usd`); shipped but unmeasured (`status:released`); open Decisions at or past 75 % of `[decisions] sla_hours` in business hours of `[time]`, with the deadline and whether it is overdue; `decision:provisional` Decisions, with their ratify-or-revert hours "n/a until WP5.6"; cost summed from the actions log. Proof coverage reads "n/a until M4" and streak "n/a until M5". Without arguments `status` rolls up every open Goal (`type:goal`). It writes nothing unless `--apply` (new, pinned to false for MCP), which posts the replace-in-place `<!-- aayudha:status -->` Goal comment and closes a Feature whose children are all released, measured, done, or cancelled and that has a Result; Initiatives and Goals close on `measure` (n/a until WP7.2). `--digest` pages the founder digest through the WP1.6 `[notify]` sender, deduplicated per repository per day; the MCP resource `aayudha://status/<n>` (WP3.3) reads `status --json`. `status --all` prints one row per `[fleet]` repository with its first open Goal's rollup
