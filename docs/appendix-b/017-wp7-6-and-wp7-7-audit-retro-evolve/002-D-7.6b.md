# D-7.6b

## Plan assumption

WP7.6 `retro`: estimate vs observed checkpoints, correction rate, streaks, floor misses, cost per story; files findings as Feedback issues on `agentic-ayudha`, never PRs; inputs in the header

## Finding

The plan fixes no checkpoint budget per Effort, no correction-rate threshold, no home for the report, and no rule for when a finding is new

## Implemented instead

`retro` reuses `audit`'s inputs and fetch (no rule suites, so no admin token) and writes `retro/<date>/retro-<cursor>.md` on the ledger with the same header, segment table, and GitHub inputs snapshot (`--replay` likewise). Per story delivered in the window: Effort, checkpoint budget (proposed: XS 1, S 2, M 3, L 5, one checkpoint per footprint layer as WP5.4 plans), checkpoints observed (the largest N of `k/N` in its packs), corrections of its merge PR, cost (`cost_usd` of its records in the segments), and proof. Agent merges, corrected merges, per-skill rates (agent PRs that changed `skills/<skill>/`), and WP5.7 streaks. Findings, each filed once as a Feedback issue (`type:feedback`, `status:backlog`, `intake`, the human-fallback tag on day one; issue-form body) titled `Feedback: retro: <finding>` and matched by title on later runs: a story over its checkpoint budget, a story over `[budget] per_story_usd`, a story merged without proof after the merge gate existed, a floor miss, and a correction rate above 20 % overall or per skill. Pre-gate merges are a founder decision, not a finding. With `retro` implemented, the WP7.1 loops render its weekly job (`aayudha-loops.yml`, `loops/amp/retro.json`, the Cursor automation); in Actions it runs with `--ci` and needs the App token to write (D1), so until the founder creates the App that job exits 4 and the Amp loop (founder identity) is the one that runs. Tested by `TestRetroFilesFeedback` (one new Feedback issue, one already filed, no PR, and a replay that files nothing)
