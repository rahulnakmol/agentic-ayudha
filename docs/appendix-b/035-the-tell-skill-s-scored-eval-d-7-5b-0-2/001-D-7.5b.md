# D-7.5b

## Plan assumption

WP7.5 and the founder's skill bar (2026-10-17): every skill in scope scores at least 4.0 of 5 (mean over k = 5, judge gpt-6.1-sol, the `evals/eval.toml` harness) with no deterministic floor failure; a new group starts advisory and gates only after two consecutive full runs at 4.0 or above, proposed in its own PR

## Finding

`tell` had no scored eval (before: none). `aayu tell --from` checked only links (a work item linked must be a proven Feature) and single avoided words, so copy could name an unproven Feature without linking it ("Team workspaces are coming soon"), describe a proven one without its proof link, call itself "the fastest", or use the exclamation marks a brand avoids, and pass. Tier 2 runs only where the `ai-gateway` environment allows (dev, rolling, main; D-3.4a), so the cases merge before their first tier-2 run

## Implemented instead

`tell.CheckDraft` (what `aayu tell --from` now runs; the repository's site pages and `release`'s generated notes keep `tell.Check`): no superlative (`tell.Superlatives`, and "most" or "least" with a quality only a comparison proves); no exclamation mark when the brand's avoid list names them; no sentence that names a Feature without authoritative packs, linked or not (a sentence names a Feature when it has every content word of its title, plurals folded, within a run of three words more than the title has, so "Note: the file holds the claims" does not name Claim notes); a proven Feature the copy names is linked at least once; release, changelog, and launch copy links a proven Feature when there is one. Paraphrases ("reassign claims in bulk" for Bulk reassignment) are left to the judge: a stemmer would refuse more honest sentences than it catches. Eval: a `tell` case type (`evals/README.md`; `internal/evals/tell.go`): the skill gets `aayu tell`'s brief, the case's own `design/brand.yaml`, and the grounding as untrusted data, writes the copy, revises once on `CheckDraft` and `scope` findings, and scores 1 without the judge if it still fails; three cases on one grounding (`evals/cases/tell/grounding.json`, Benefits QA: proven #7, #15, #16; unproven #9 with a shipped story on an advisory pack, #20 with none): `release-notes` (v1.5.0 notes that describe #15 and #16 from their packs and count the other two changes), `guide-export` (a how-to for #7 that names only the controls its criteria name), and `launch-saved-filters` (a request that also asks for #20, "the fastest", and hours saved; `scope` rules for a time saving and a hint at an unproven Feature). Rubrics: every claim restates a met criterion, a Feature field, or a Result; the voice matches the case's brand. Group `tell` (8 USD, `gate = "advisory"`, dispatch only). The skill body gains the claims rules, the brand's tone words as visible choices, and a shape per kind, with examples outside the eval's fixture. Tests: `TestCheckDraft`, `TestTier1TellRules`, `TestTier2TellRevisesOnceThenJudges`, `TestGroups`. Tier-2 runs and their scores follow in D-7.5c onwards
