# D-3.4r

## Plan assumption

D-3.4o: two consecutive full host-skills runs at a mean of 4.0 for every case

## Finding

The first full run of the pair, [39035045698](https://github.com/rahulnakmol/agentic-ayudha/actions/runs/39035045698) on c538e3b (5.97 USD at list price), held every case's mean (`build/small-story` 4.0, `build/unasked-pickup` 4.4, `ready/readback-pass` 4.8, `ready/refuse-unready` 4.8, `verify/map-drift` 4.67 on its scored runs, `verify/preview-honest` 4.6) and had no floor failure, but failed on `verify/map-drift`'s two errors: both skill replies looped on `<br>` until the 32000-token cap, one from its first token and one after a few lines of its report. With the one in run 39032651481 that is three such replies in thirteen map-drift runs, from a session that asks for no HTML, so the one error a case may have cannot absorb it. Who was wrong: the harness. D-3.4o kept a reply at the cap as an error because a retry under the same cap would stop there again; that holds for a reply that is long, not for one that repeats a single token, where a new sample is the call retried, as for a 5xx

## Implemented instead

A reply that stops at the cap with its last 2000 characters one repeated unit of at most 64 characters (`repeating` in `internal/evals/tier2.go`) is sampled once more; the call records `Retried: degenerate reply: N tokens repeating "<br>"`, and both calls are paid for. A long reply at the cap that does not repeat is still not retried and stays an error (`TestTier2RetriesServerErrors`). A retry repeats a call, never a judged run, and no skill, case, floor, or rubric changed. The pair of full runs restarts on this commit
