# D-3.4e

## Plan assumption

WP3.4: "pass^k ≥ 0.8 and mean rubric score ≥ 3.5 to pass; a drop of more than 0.3 mean or any pass^k fall below baseline fails the promotion"

## Finding

The harness applied pass^k ≥ 0.8 to every case as well as the suite. One case's pass^5 is 0 or 1, so a single off run in five failed the promotion gate: the rolling candidates 662cd99 and 349d52c each failed on a different skills case (plan 5 2 5 5 5, then slice at mean 4.20) while the suite stood at pass^5 0.80 and mean 4.80

## Implemented instead

pass^k is enforced at the suite (group) level, as the plan states it; per case it is printed as an advisory. Still enforced per case: the mean bar, the error limit, and any fall below the case's baseline pass^k. Each WP's own acceptance (WP5.3, 5.4, 7.3) still quotes its per-case numbers.
