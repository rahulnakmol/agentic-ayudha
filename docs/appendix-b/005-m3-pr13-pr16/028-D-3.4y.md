# D-3.4y

## Plan assumption

WP3.4 and D-3.4o/r: at most one run of a case may error (`MaxErrors`); a provider fault was retried once per call (a dropped connection, a 5xx, a stream server error, a reply looping on one token), and anything else, such as a stalled stream or a 429, errored the run

## Finding

In the host-skills rounds that led to D-3.4x, provider faults, not skills, used up the one error a case may have: a reply that looped on `<br>` twice in a row ([39055371933](https://github.com/rahulnakmol/agentic-ayudha/actions/runs/39055371933)), a stream that stalled past the client's 10-minute timeout ([39038277548](https://github.com/rahulnakmol/agentic-ayudha/actions/runs/39038277548)), and the 502 and 524 storms before D-3.4o. Founder decision (2026-10-17, "Retrying provider outages: Yes"): provider faults should not count against a skill. Retry them separately, record each retry and its cost in the results, and cap the retries per sample so an outage cannot burn the budget; past the cap the sample is an error as before. Model-quality failures, floors, and malformed answers are unchanged, and this applies to every group

## Implemented instead

`Harness.call` (`internal/evals/tier2.go`) retries a call on a provider fault (`providerFault`): a dropped connection, a 5xx (the gateway's 524 included) or a 429 (after 10 s times the retry's number), a stream `api_error` or `overloaded_error`, a stream that stalled past the client's timeout (a `net.Error` timeout while the harness's context is live), or a reply at the cap that loops on one short unit. A run (one of a case's k samples) may retry at most `ProviderRetries` = 2 faults across all its calls; the next fault returns as before and errors the run. Each call records `retries`: every fault it was repeated after, with model, tokens, and `cost_usd`, which is charged to the group's `max_usd` like any call (the `retried` string field is gone). A 4xx other than 429, a long reply at the cap that does not repeat, malformed JSON, and every floor and judgement are unchanged. `TestTier2RetriesProviderFaults` drives a fake provider through a 429, a stall, and a 524 then a `<br>` loop, each followed by a good reply (each run scores; the loop's retry records its 0.3 USD), and three overloaded streams in one run (an error, and the case's only one). `MaxErrors`, `pass_score`, and every bar are unchanged, and the eval-bar ratchet passes
