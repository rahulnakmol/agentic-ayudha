# D-6.7a

## Plan assumption

WP6.7: `experiment` and `readout` on GrowthBook + PostHog, with a minimum sample size, writing Results; accept: fixture lift computed, under-sample refused

## Finding

Built ahead of M6 (founder scheduling, 2026-10-12) with no PostHog or GrowthBook account. Integration keys are read-only (WP1.3), so the binary cannot create the feature flag the registry lists as an `experiment` output. GrowthBook's REST API has no endpoint that reports a key's role. The plan names no statistical test, metric type, or Result format, and WP2.8's "a readout/Result exists" does not say where the readout lives

## Implemented instead

`internal/experiment` holds the statistics, all deterministic (rule 7): the per-arm minimum sample of a two-sided two-proportion z-test, `n = (z(1-α/2)·√(2p̄(1-p̄)) + z(1-β)·√(p1(1-p1)+p2(1-p2)))² / (p2-p1)²` rounded up, and a readout of two arms (pooled z, two-sided p, unpooled 1-α confidence interval for the difference); provider statistics are never used. `experiment <feature> --hypothesis --metric --flag --mde [--exposure --alpha --power --days]` (new registry inputs; parent a Feature or Initiative, D-2.1a) takes the baseline rate from two reproducible PostHog baselines over the last `--days` whole UTC days (persons in the `--exposure` population, and those among them who sent `--metric`), refuses a zero population (baseline gate, exit 4), computes the size from `--mde` (absolute `0.02` or relative `10%`), alpha 0.05 and power 0.8 by default, and files an Experiment (`status:planned`) as a sub-issue whose Baseline lists both tuples and whose Minimum sample is `N per arm; two-sided two-proportion z-test, alpha …, power …, baseline rate …, detectable rate …`; a second open Experiment on the same flag under the same parent is refused. The flag is created in PostHog or GrowthBook by a human. `readout <experiment> [--source posthog\|growthbook] [--metric] [--from]` re-derives the size from the Minimum sample parameters and refuses (exit 4, nothing written) when the stated size differs (the body is untrusted, §0.1) or when either arm has fewer users than the size (the min-sample gate). PostHog counts come from one HogQL query over the `$feature/<flag>` event property, grouped by variant, from `--from` (default the Experiment's creation) to now, with `control` as the control arm; the flag key and event name are refused unless they match a strict pattern, since they reach HogQL from the issue body. GrowthBook: `[integrations.growthbook]` (new adapter `internal/integrations/growthbook`, doctor reachability through `GET /api/v1/experiments?limit=1`; the configured read scope is trusted and doctor says so) finds the experiment by tracking key (= the flag key) and reads the metric's frequentist numerator and denominator per variation from the latest results, the first variation as control; a metric that is not a conversion (numerator above denominator or not whole) is refused. Readout compares exactly two arms. It sets `status:measured` first (so the Ready guard has no `status:ready` to strip on the body edit), then writes the Result: relative and absolute lift, the verdict at alpha, z, p, the interval, a per-arm table, the window, and the exact source query. `status`'s parent-close rule now also accepts an Experiment child with a Result as the Feature's readout. Fixtures are synthetic and labelled so; the hand-worked sizes (3841 for 0.10 → 0.12 at 0.05 and 0.8; 10921 for 0.05 → 0.06 at 0.05 and 0.9) and lifts (PostHog +20 %, z 2.8748, p 0.00404; GrowthBook −10 %, z −1.8879, p 0.0590) are in the tests. `TestLiveReadout` runs behind `AAYU_LIVE=1` and skips without `AAYU_SECRET_GROWTHBOOK` or the PostHog settings
