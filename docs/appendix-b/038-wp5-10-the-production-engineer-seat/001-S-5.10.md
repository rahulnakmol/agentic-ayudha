# S-5.10

## Plan assumption

WP5.1 and S-5.1g: thirteen seats; the AI Native Engineer wears an "SRE hat", owns L07, and is Accountable for `respond`; `ops/**` is in L04 with the Quality Architect

## Finding

Founder decisions (2026-10-17, relayed by the orchestrating thread): production engineering, the agentic running of software in production, is a key capability, and it is its own seat with a persona and a Greek-named twin, not a hat: the AI Native Engineer builds, the new seat runs. Reference practice: the founder's Opsci advisor (`rahulnakmol/ampcode`, `plugins/advisory-team/opsci.md` and `shared.md`), adapted into aayu's formats, not pasted. The design was settled first as Decision #324

## Implemented instead

**Fourteen seats.** `verbs.Seats` gains `production-engineer` ("Production Engineer", twin Atlas, proposed for the founder's veto as D-5.1e) after the AI Native Engineer, whose name drops "(+SRE hat)". `seats/production-engineer/persona.toml` has five sourced practices: SLOs and error budgets, Incident command and blameless postmortems, and Safe rollouts (moved unchanged from the AI Native Engineer), Investigation from telemetry (new, from Opsci: Investigation note, Incident assessment, Investigation record), and Runbooks and toil (new: Runbook entry, Runbook set, Operational readiness review). The AI Native Engineer keeps Observability first and gains Small verifiable checkpoints and Tests before code (a persona needs three practices); its SRE habits, questions, and "never" entries move to Atlas. Layers: L07 moves to the new seat and `ops/**` moves from L04 into L07 (the catalog, SLOs, and runbooks are what the seat runs production with; L04 keeps the Quality Architect's floors, proof, and `CONSTRAINTS.md`); the AI Native Engineer owns no layer, as the Product Strategist and the Revenue Architect own none (`verbs.LintRACI` holds one owner per layer). `respond`'s Accountable seat becomes the Production Engineer, Consulted AI Native Engineer and DevOps; the Production Engineer is Consulted on `build`, `plan`, and `release`. `decide.OwningSeat` routes incidents, SLOs, runbooks, rollbacks, postmortems, telemetry, and alerts, and Incident issues, to the new seat. Every charter's first line, `seats/README.md`, the AGENTS.md seat sentence, GLOSSARY.md, and the guide's seat count say fourteen.
