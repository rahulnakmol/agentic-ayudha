# D-5.10c

## Plan assumption

Decision #324: `respond` gains a postmortem from the timeline and files the follow-up Bug; prove it end to end on a realistic fixture

## Finding

A postmortem's evidence is spread over the incident: its body, the timeline comment, and the investigation note; the follow-up Bug `--close` filed carried only a pointer to the timeline; an end-to-end run needs a real deploy, real telemetry, and an executed catalog action, which a unit test cannot hold

## Implemented instead

**Checked, blameless postmortem; the Bug carries the hand-off.** `aayu respond <incident> --postmortem <file>` checks the draft (`production.CheckPostmortem`: the stage's artefact, Incident log, Postmortem, or Incident review, from the Incident command practice; Summary, Impact, Timeline citing the incident with UTC times, Causes naming the root cause and the contributing conditions, What went well, Actions with owners and their Bug or Story; the evidence rule resolved against the incident, its timeline, and its investigation note; and blameless language, refusing blame words and "@person forgot/broke/caused") and posts it as one comment replaced in place, with a timeline line. `--close` refuses a Sev0 or Sev1 incident without a posted postmortem, and the Bug it files carries the investigation note's Implicated code and Durable fix (`production.HandOff`). `TestIncidentEndToEnd` (`integration` tag, so the rolling and stable gates run it; skipped on Windows) builds a scratch product from `examples/benefits-qa`, deploys R1 and R2 with `deploy/deploy.sh` (R2's tie-break reads `ranked[1]` and panics on single-match questions), drives real traffic, and runs `investigate` on the real export (the deployed revision, the previous one, the changed commit, and the panicking line), posts a checked note, proposes `rollback-benefits-qa`, executes it through `respond --ci` with the Environment approval simulated, verifies recovery with a bounded query over the window since the rollback, records the run in the timeline, posts the postmortem, and closes with the Bug (`TestRespondPostmortem`, `TestCheckPostmortem`)
