# The mission

## The mission

Make agent work provable and keep a person in charge of the calls that matter.
Every work item starts as a clear GitHub issue with numbered promises.
Every pull request carries proof that CI writes, not the agent.
A person accepts Decisions, publishes, and holds the brake.
Teams adopt it at their own pace, in three levels.
Each level is a complete place to stop.

## How we know it works

aayu builds itself with its own process.
Initiative #30 measures proof coverage on aayu's own Stories.
Proof coverage is the share of done Stories with an authoritative pack that holds.
The target is 100 %.
See [[Prove every Story of aayu itself]] and [[Proof, not claims]].
