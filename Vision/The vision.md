# The vision

## The vision

A coding agent can write a feature in an afternoon.
aayu is the delivery team around that agent.
It turns a vague idea into clear promises.
It checks every change against those promises before the change merges.
Then it measures whether the result helped.
It answers each step with evidence, from a fuzzy idea to a number that moved.

## Who it serves

It serves one or two people who build a product with coding agents.
They cannot hold every role at once.
aayu gives them fourteen seats, and an agent twin does the work of each seat.
The person stays the one who decides.
See [[The solo founder]] and [[A person paired with a seat]].

## What it is not

aayu is not a coding agent.
It does not run your production.
It organises, checks, and records the work that your agent does.
It works inside the agent you already use, on your GitHub repository.
