# Start here

This vault is the product brain of rahulnakmol/agentic-ayudha. It keeps the product knowledge that must outlive any one person.
Issues stay the record of the work. The notes here link to issues and do not copy them.

## The path for a new person

1. [[Vision]]: why the product exists, and its mission.
2. [[Principles]]: the rules we keep.
3. [[Personas]] and [[Problems]]: who we serve, and what gets in their way.
4. [[Direction]] and [[Objectives]]: where we go, and how we measure it.
5. [[Capabilities]]: what the product does, and why.
6. [[Decisions]] and [[Objections]]: what we chose, and the cases against it.
7. [[Releases]] and [[Timeline]]: how the product got here.
8. [[Glossary]], [[Guidance]], [[Seats]], and [[People]]: the words, the ways of work, and the people.

## How this vault works

Open the brain branch as a folder in Obsidian. The graph view colours each note by its type.
People and twins write the notes. `aayu brain checkpoint` refreshes only the marked generated blocks.
Every note names its sources. `aayu brain check` finds broken links, missing sources, and long sentences.
