# Work items and Ready

## What it does

Work is a GitHub issue of one of ten types, such as Goal, Feature, Story, and Bug.
Issue forms and labels give each type its shape.
A Story or Bug carries a Completion Goal of numbered criteria.
aayu ready checks an item and posts a Ready snapshot.
The snapshot freezes what the people agreed.

## Why it exists

Promises that change quietly cannot be proven.
The Ready guard notices when a Ready body drifts.

## Who it serves

It serves anyone who files or picks up work, from Level 1 of adoption.
Level 1 needs no keys and no CI changes.
See [[Ready (term)]] and [[Completion Goal (term)]].
