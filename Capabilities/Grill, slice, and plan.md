# Grill, slice, and plan

## What it does

grill interviews a person, one question at a time.
It turns a vague ask into a work item with numbered promises.
slice breaks a Feature into Stories with layer footprints and dependencies.
plan writes how a Story will be built, and the Engineering Manager reviews risky plans.

## Why it exists

"Make it faster" cannot be proven.
A Goal with a baseline and Stories with criteria can.

## Who it serves

It serves the person with the idea, and the twins that build from it.
See [[Completion Goal (term)]].
