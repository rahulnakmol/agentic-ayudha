# Tell and brief

## What it does

brief explains work to each audience in its register, such as eli5, ste, or engineer.
It grounds its claims in proof packs.
tell drafts docs and launch copy, and checks each claim.
Only a person publishes.

## Why it exists

Proven work still needs to be understood.
A claim without a pack behind it does not ship.

## Who it serves

It serves readers outside the pod, and the Mentalist and Marketer & Documenter seats.
