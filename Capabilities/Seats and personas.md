# Seats and personas

## What it does

aayu gives a pod fourteen seats, from Product Strategist to Founder.
Each seat has an agent twin with a persona: a voice, values, and a practice playbook.
Each verb has one Accountable seat, and each layer has one owner seat.
A seat runs solo, or paired with a person.

## Why it exists

One or two people cannot hold every role at once.
A seat outlives the person who fills it.

## Who it serves

It serves a solo founder first, then a team that grows.
See [[Seat (term)]], [[Twin (term)]], and [[A seat for production engineering]].
