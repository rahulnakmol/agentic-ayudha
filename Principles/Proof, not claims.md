# Proof, not claims

## The principle

Work is done when a proof pack shows every Completion Goal criterion met with evidence.
CI writes the pack, not the agent that did the work.

## Why

"It works on my machine" is not evidence.
"The model says it is fine" is not evidence either.
The agent that did the work must not mark its own work.

## In practice

A pack maps each promise in a Story to its evidence for one exact commit.
Ship reads the pack before it merges.
See [[Proof pack (term)]] and [[Proof packs and floors]].
