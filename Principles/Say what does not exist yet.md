# Say what does not exist yet

## The principle

A feature that a later work package will build is "n/a until WPx.y".
Nothing pretends to be done.

## Why

A false "done" hides risk from the people who decide.
A plain "not yet" is honest.

## In practice

Ground rule 1a of the build plan sets this rule.
doctor reports such a feature as not yet available.
It never reports it as a failure.
