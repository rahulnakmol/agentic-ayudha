# Pay for what you use

## The principle

Budgets cap model spend and CI spend.
Expensive floors run only where the change can affect them.
The breaker pages a person instead of spending more.

## Why

Agents can spend without limit if nothing stops them.
A person must know before the spend grows.

## In practice

Level 1 of adoption needs no keys and no CI minutes.
Each level adds cost only when you choose it.
